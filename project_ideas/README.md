

Idea 1 — Anime Popularity vs Ratings
Argument: Just because the  anime is popular does not mean it's  always the highest quality.

Dataset sources
Kaggle Anime Dataset (MyAnimeList data)
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/unibahmad/anime-dataset
Anime ratings, genres, episodes, popularity


Research questions
Do the most popular anime actually have the highest ratings?
What  genres receive higher ratings?



Visualizations ideas
Scatterplot: popularity vs rating
Bar chart: average rating by genre



Idea 2 Fast Food Nutrition vs Marketing
Argument: The more marketed the fast food item is , the more unhealthy it is .

Dataset ideas
Fast food nutrition datasets (Kaggle)
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ulrikthygepedersen/fastfood-nutrition

Research questions
Which fast food chains have the highest calorie items?
Are the most popular menu items = most unhealthy?



Visualizations ideas
Bar chart: average calories by chain
Heatmap of nutrition metrics
Ranking of healthiest vs unhealthiest item
